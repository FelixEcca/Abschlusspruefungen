## Prompt-Set

Gemeinsame Vorgaben fuer alle Motive:

Use case: identity-preserve. Preserve Gisela's friendly gender-neutral adult
golden giraffe face, very long spotted neck, teal round glasses, two ossicones
and warm intelligent expression. Refined hand-drawn 2D animation style with soft
warm cel shading and confident dark brown outlines. Full-body upright anatomy:
exactly two hind legs and two articulated forelimbs ending in split hooves,
no human fingers and no extra limbs. Clothing must be genuinely tailored with
natural folds, not a flat panel pasted on the body. Portrait approximately
1024 x 1536, full figure and props within generous transparent margins. True
alpha transparency, no backdrop, captions, logos, watermark or checkerboard.
Use the original Gisela as identity reference and the new workshop Gisela as
the anatomy/style reference for the other five motifs.

metal slider, no gibberish display digits. Experienced and approachable.

## Animation

Each illustration has individually fitted head, eye, tail and prop masks.
Blink every five seconds; short nod and restrained prop presentation every
thirty seconds; continuous gentle tail movement. The machine stays stationary
and the laboratory gesture is especially small to keep the test tubes upright.
Reduced-motion preferences show the unmodified, still illustration.
